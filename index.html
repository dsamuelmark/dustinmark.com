Creative Services

What I can
do for you.

A comprehensive menu of creative services. All offered with full professionalism and a complete understanding that most of these inquiries won't pan out. That's okay.

01
Full-Time Employment
Broad Ask

Hire me as a real, salaried employee with benefits and everything. I will attend most meetings and occasionally have strong opinions about the subject line.

Pricing: Negotiable. Benefits appreciated. Snacks preferred.

02
Feature Screenplay
Long-Form

A complete 95–115 page feature film script with actual three-act structure, character arcs, and scene descriptions that are technically correct.

Pricing: Let's talk. WGA rates are public information.

03
Novel
Long-Form

80,000–100,000 words of literary fiction. Can accommodate most genres except one. Up to 33% may be in Danish.

Pricing: Significant. Worth it. Probably.

04
Podcast Series
Audio

Narrative audio content from someone who has actually produced four narrative podcast series. I know things now I wish I'd known then.

Pricing: Per-episode or series rate available.

05
Greeting Card
Short-Form ✦

One (1) sentiment appropriate for a life event. Suitable for framing, gifting, or immediate recycling. Bulk orders welcome—pricing improves.

Pricing: Let's be honest, this one should be cheap.

06
Corporate Copy
Commercial

Marketing copy that will make your brand seem relatable, thoughtful, and vaguely aware of current events. Results guaranteed until scrutinized.

Pricing: Market rate plus the surcharge for caring.

07
Best Man / Toast
Special Occasion

7–10 minutes of remarks that will make the groom briefly regret asking, then deeply grateful. Ceremony-appropriate unless specified otherwise.

Pricing: Flat fee. Worth every dollar at the reception.

08
Humor Consultation
Advisory

30 minutes during which I will tell you if something is funny. I will not be polite about it. Bring a thick skin and your best material.

Pricing: Hourly. I will not go over.

09
Voiceover Work
Audio

A voice with four narrative podcasts behind it and a studio setup that sounds like a choice rather than a compromise. Deadpan available. Warmth also available.

Pricing: Rate card available. Demo on request.

10
Eulogy
Special Occasion ✦

Remarks that honor the person while not being unbearable to sit through. If they were funny, I'll make that clear. If they weren't, I'll find something true.

Pricing: Sliding scale. Context-dependent.

11
Children's Book
Short-Form

A picture book for children, written so the adult reading it out loud doesn't lose the will to live by page three. Age-appropriate throughout. Mostly.

Pricing: Reasonable. Rhymes cost more.

The guy
behind the

Humor writer, editor, and multimedia creator with over 15 years of published comedy and conceptual work. Contributor to McSweeney's Internet Tendency and Slackjaw. Based in Denver, CO.

B.A. in English from the University of Texas at Austin 😉. Former Associate Editor at Texas Travesty, the collegiate humor publication. Current-ish assistant manager at a recreational sports facility — which, against all expectations, has prepared him for most of this.

The Dustin Mark Project is an ongoing narrative multimedia work spanning four podcasts and two books, following a fictional character through an expanding set of personas.
